# Founder Documents Checklist — what to prepare before starting

**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Purpose:** the documents an entrepreneur in this business needs, grouped by what they actually decide. Ordered so the ones that can **invalidate the plan** come first.
**Evidence base:** nine research files in this folder. `[UNVERIFIED]` means the underlying research could not confirm it.

> **Read §0 first.** Four items can each kill the business on their own, and all four are cheap to resolve.

## 0. The four kill-switches — resolve before spending anything else

| #   | Question                                                                                  | Why it can kill you                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Cost to resolve                                  | Owner   |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| 1   | **Does your chosen state require nursery registration/licensing?**                        | Telangana Registration of Horticulture Nurseries (Regulation) Act 2010, Haryana Horticulture Nurseries Act 2025, West Bengal Act XXXI of 2001 and the Uttarakhand Fruit Nurseries Act all require registration to operate a nursery. **This makes city selection a legal decision, not just a density decision.** | One call to the State Horticulture Directorate   | Founder |
| 2   | **Does interstate movement of live ornamental plants need a plant movement certificate?** | **Unresolved across all our research.** The PQ Order 2003 governs _import_ under the Destructive Insects & Pests Act 1914; domestic movement appears to be state territory. No uniform requirement verified, no small-retail exemption found.                                                                     | One call to State Horticulture + PPQS in writing | Founder |
| 3   | **What does a real hyperlocal courier charge per drop in your zone?**                     | Only India Post Speed Post publishes a full Indian rate card. Blue Dart, Ecom, Shadowfax, XpressBees, Ekart, DTDC publish **no rupee rates at all**. Our entire contribution model rests on an assumed ₹45–70 per drop.                                                                                           | Three quote calls                                | Founder |
| 4   | **What is FSSAI's position on potting soil / compost / growing media?**                   | Genuinely unresolved. Ornamental plants are clearly outside FSSAI (§2(d): "intended for human consumption"), but growing media is the grey area. Our refill line depends on it.                                                                                                                                   | Written query to FSSAI                           | Founder |

**Do not order stock, sign a lease, or publish a catalogue until all four have answers.**

## 1. Strategy & market — the thinking documents

These are the documents that define the business. Most founders skip them and go straight to a logo.

| #   | Document                              | Purpose                                                         | Must answer                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1.1 | **Business Model Canvas**             | One page, nine blocks. The single most-used strategic artefact. | Forces you to state who pays, what costs, and what you do that others don't                                                                                                                        |
| 1.2 | **Vision & Mission statement**        | What exists in 5 years, and why you exist beyond profit         | _"The only plant seller in [city] that answers on WhatsApp, tells you when it arrives, and replaces it if it dies in 90 days — and has never touched a courier box."_                              |
| 1.3 | **Market & customer research report** | Who actually buys, and why                                      | Your buyer is **not** the gifting buyer (you gift someone else, once). FNP/IGP structurally cannot capture repeat purchase — that demand is yours                                                  |
| 1.4 | **Competitive positioning map**       | Where you sit against nine researched competitors               | 2×2: **own-fleet vs courier** × **service-led vs price-led**. Urvann owns "own fleet but service-poor". Ugaoo owns "courier, broad, spec-heavy". The empty quadrant is **own fleet + service-led** |
| 1.5 | **Target customer persona**           | 2–3 real profiles                                               | Must reflect India's actual buying reality: **rented flat, no natural light, AC on, north-facing window.** A "how bright is your window?" question false-negatives most of the urban Indian market |
| 1.6 | **City selection scorecard**          | Which single city, and why                                      | Weighted on: density (12 km zone population), nursery licensing regime (**kill-switch #1**), rent, climate band, courier coverage, existing plant-demand signal                                    |
| 1.7 | **Seasonal & climate calendar**       | Your operating rhythm                                           | **March–June at 40–46°C is the lethal season.** Summer/monsoon/perennial taxonomy. Substitute hardy SKUs — **never pause, never ship into lethal conditions**                                      |

## 2. Financial — the numbers that decide survival

| #   | Document                                | Purpose                                     | Key content                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2.1 | **Unit economics model**                | The most important spreadsheet you'll build | Per-order: COGS, delivery, packaging, gateway, RTO, DOA reserve → **contribution margin**. Benchmark: **₹91/order at 14.1%, break-even 660 orders/month.** Our model target: ₹200–260 at ~25–30% |
| 2.2 | **Sensitivity analysis**                | Which lever actually matters                | **AOV +₹100 = +₹45 contribution** (the winner). Freight −20% = +₹24. Both = ₹160, break-even 375/mo. **Festive COD spike = −₹60, worse than either lever can beat**                              |
| 2.3 | **12–18 month cashflow & runway model** | When do you run out                         | Must include a 2–3 day pre-dispatch drying buffer if you ever ship, and the density ramp. **[ASSUMED]** fixed cost ₹60,000/month — replace with your real numbers                                |
| 2.4 | **Capital requirement & use-of-funds**  | How much, for how long                      | **Honest answer from our research: ₹15–25 lakh is enough to build Urvann's asset base.** He built what he did with ~₹5 Cr over four years, using off-the-shelf SaaS                              |
| 2.5 | **Working capital model**               | The killer for live plants                  | **A returned plant is a total write-off** — non-saleable in every policy we examined, and needs 1–2 months to restore. Stock is cash you cannot get back                                         |
| 2.6 | **Pricing architecture**                | The revenue lever                           | Planters/pots at **+29–121% uplift, largest at the small end**. Ugaoo: ₹199 → ₹1,099 for an identical plant. Set free-shipping threshold as a deliberate AOV target                              |
| 2.7 | **SKU & catalogue economics**           | What to grow                                | Include the Urvann insight: ~30% of its SKUs are plastic pots because **"a ₹149 plant is a loss leader that exists to sell a ₹329 cocopeat bag"**                                                |

### 3.2 Documents that are genuinely _not_ required

State these positively so you don't waste money on consultants telling you otherwise.

| Not needed                            | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **FSSAI licence (ornamental plants)** | §2(d) FSS Act defines food as _"intended for human consumption."_ A décor plant fails that test. Blogs claiming you need it are wrong on the statute. **Reassess only if you add edible plants, vegetable seedlings, sprouts, or microgreens** |
| CITES / phytosanitary import permit   | Only for importing planting material from abroad. **We don't import** — the Post Entry Quarantine facility-certificate path is disproportionate for a startup                                                                                  |
| Drug/Drugs & Cosmetics licence        | Unless you sell a plant-derived preparation with a health claim                                                                                                                                                                                |
| CIB&RC licence                        | Only to _manufacture_ pesticides. You may resell approved products; you may not manufacture or re-label                                                                                                                                        |
| Any central "plant sale" licence      | Does not exist. The relevant regimes are state nursery registration (3.1.8) and plant quarantine for import/movement                                                                                                                           |

| 3.3.2 | **Return, Cancellation & Refund Policy**    | Consumer Protection Act 2019 + E-Commerce Rules 2020                                            |
| 3.3.3 | **Shipping & Delivery Policy**              | E-Commerce Rules 2020                                                                           |

| 3.3.5 | **Grievance Officer appointment + contact** | E-Commerce Rules 2020 — mandatory platform disclosure                                           |
| 3.3.6 | **Legal Metrology labelling spec**          | **PC Rules 2011 r.6(1) + 2017 Amendment r.6(10)**                                               |
| 3.3.7 | **Plant Guarantee / Warranty terms**        | Your differentiator. One replacement per plant, ever                                            |
| 3.3.8 | Grievance redressal mechanism               | E-Commerce Rules 2020                                                                           |

### 3.4 Legal Metrology — the obligation everyone misses

**A potted plant shipped in a carton, and especially a bag of soil, IS a pre-packaged commodity by definition.** Buyer absent, packaged, pre-determined quantity.

Mandatory declarations — **on the physical package AND on your online listing** (only month-and-year-of-packing is exempt online):

- Name & address of manufacturer/packer/importer
- Country of origin (imports)
- Common/generic name of the commodity
- Net quantity or number of units
- Best-before / use-by date, if it can become unfit
- **MRP inclusive of all taxes**
- **Dimensions, where size is relevant — i.e. every single plant PDP**

Also: increased minimum legible font, and **no dual MRP** on identical goods.

**Build this into the PDP template before you have 200 SKUs.** It's a design constraint, not a retrofit.

## 4. Operations

| #    | Document                             | Purpose                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4.1  | **Nursery layout & production plan** | What you grow, in what volume, on what cycle. 1–2 acres in-city (Urvann's proven scale)                                                                        |
| 4.2  | **Plant care protocols**             | Internal reference: light, water, temperature, toxicity per species. Not customer-facing marketing — actual horticultural SOP                                  |
| 4.3  | **Sourcing & supplier SOP**          | Who you buy from, traceability, quality standards. **Rule: never list a species you can't trace to a bill**                                                    |
| 4.4  | **Packing SOP**                      | Even at zero-packaging, you need a dispatch protocol. **Never ship wet** — moisture rots, leaks, and _couriers reject wet boxes_; 2–3 days pre-dispatch drying |
| 4.5  | **Delivery SOP**                     | Routes, time windows, POD, failure handling. **Mon–Wed for inter-state lanes**                                                                                 |
| 4.6  | **Rider incentive design**           | ⭐ **The document that decides whether you become Urvann.** Per-drop pricing with a distance band, so lying never pays                                         |
| 4.7  | **Damage/DOA adjudication SOP**      | Evidence required, timelines, replacement policy, fraud review gate. Bind your customer claim window to the courier's (7 days is Paudhewale's precedent)       |
| 4.8  | **Quality control checklist**        | Pre-dispatch inspection. Photograph at the packing bench against the order ID                                                                                  |
| 4.9  | **Inventory & quarantine SOP**       | Especially if sourcing interstate. Stock is non-recoverable cash                                                                                               |
| 4.10 | **Technology stack list**            | Storefront (Mystore/Shopify-class — **no tech moat exists**), payment gateway, CRM, WhatsApp Business API, rider app                                           |
| 4.11 | **Insurance**                        | Consider transit/cargo cover. Note: own-rider delivery changes the risk profile                                                                                |

## 10. What you do NOT need to prepare

Stated to save money and time:

| Not needed                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A care app**                         | Vera was acquired by Bloomscape with $24M raised, was free end-to-end, monetised nothing, and was delisted. **The container died; the content compounded.** Build content + a WhatsApp channel |
| **A subscription spec** (yet)          | Four mature operators, zero working plant subscriptions. Bloomscape exited with $24M raised. Build the **refill** subscription instead — no mortality risk, 70–80% margin                      |
| **Custom technology**                  | Urvann uses off-the-shelf SaaS. **There is no tech moat in this category**                                                                                                                     |
| **A pan-India logistics plan**         | Freight is 95–105% of product price for a potted plant. Urvann had expansion capital, a stated national goal, and **two metros four years later after nine failed city launches**              |
| **A no-skip / no-cancel subscription** | UPI mandate disputes make it a trust and support liability                                                                                                                                     |
| **An import/supply-chain plan**        | Post Entry Quarantine facility certification is disproportionate for a startup. Source domestically                                                                                            |
