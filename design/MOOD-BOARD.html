Warm botanical
editorially calm.

Every competitor we audited — Ugaoo, Urvann, Paudhewale, TrustBasket, Bloomscape, The Sill, Hey Rooted — reaches for the same cold, high-chroma, discount-coded green. Nobody in the set combines a warm cream ground with a deep forest ink, and nobody puts their delivery promise in the hero. This board takes the structure the winners use and the palette nobody does.

"Grown 40 minutes away. Replaced if it dies in 90 days."

Typography

Competitors split into two camps: geometric/grotesque sans (Ugaoo on Outfit, TrustBasket on Karla, Foliage Factory on Poppins) and neutral system sans (Bloomscape, The Sill). Almost nobody sets a serif display. Our current ui-serif h1 is the right instinct and stays — but it needs to be allowed to be large.

Display / h1

Grown forty minutes away.

Iowan Old Style → Palatino → Georgia. 46–64px, weight 400, tracking −0.02em. Not bold. A 600-weight serif at this size reads like a newspaper, not a nursery.

Visual direction

Four tiles. Each is a rule for how a photograph is shot and cropped, because for a plant brand the photography is the design — there is no styling to hide behind it.

01 · Hero

One plant, one light source

A single specimen against warm cream, raking daylight from one side, deep shadow. Never a flat-lay grid, never a lifestyle shot with a smiling family. We sell a plant, not a room.

02 · Colour

Terracotta is the accent

Clay appears on planters, pots, benches and the eyebrow only. Green stays reserved for truth — a serviceable answer, a confirmed state. The two never trade jobs.

03 · Ground

Cream, not white

Every surface is warm (#faf6ee / #fffdf8). Pure white next to foliage reads clinical and blows out the greens in photography.

04 · Proof

Show the bench, not the studio

Packing bench, nursery rows, a rider's open EV basket. The provenance shot is the differentiator: "40 minutes away" is a claim a national brand cannot make.

The white space

What no competitor in the set does

  1. Prints a number in the hero. Bloomscape's hero is "What kind of plant parent are you?"; The Sill's is a seasonal collection. Ours is 90 days and 15 minutes, above the fold, in the headline itself.
  2. Shows the delivery promise before the product. Every competitor leads with a carousel. Ours leads with do we deliver to you — because for an Indian first-time buyer in a rented flat that is the real question, and it is where we beat Urvann's ₹99 floor.
  3. Explains why there is no COD. Ugaoo, Paudhewale and TrustBasket all offer COD and none of them say what it costs them. Ours says it in one line — it is a feature and the research says it is the reason the category's returns are fatal.
  4. Leads on provenance, not scale. Ugaoo = 25 acres in Talegaon. Urvann = 8 farms, 500 riders, 2 cities. Ours = 2 acres, 40 minutes, one city. Distance beats size and no national brand can copy it.
  5. Uses a warm palette. Seven sites audited, all cold white or high-chroma green. Bloomscape is the only warm ground and it is the one people call premium.
  6. Says "not yet" as a feature. Paudhewale's <5% RTO is the only competitor publishing a reliability number. Nobody publishes a refusal as prominently as a promise.

✗ What we drop

  • Struck-through MRP / sale badge — every Indian site shows one; it's a merchandising fiction we refuse.
  • Sale-yellow and safety-orange accents (Ugaoo, TrustBasket) — discount-coded, wrong register.
  • A 150-item mega-nav. Our catalogue is seasonal and small; a big nav would lie about depth.
  • Pure white. Cold ground kills the photography.
  • Sticky WhatsApp bubble over the fold — we put the number in the trust rail where it can't cover a product.

✓ What we keep from the winners

  • Split hero, single specimen, generous type — The Sill, Hey Rooted.
  • Four room/light facet tiles — Bloomscape, Ugaoo.
  • A four-item trust rail with icons — Ugaoo, TrustBasket.
  • Horizontal product rail into a grid — Hey Rooted, Ugaoo, TrustBasket.
  • Care facts on the card: water interval, soil-moisture %, pet-safety, difficulty — the schema every leader has and most Indian sites hide.
  • The planter as a visible, priced variant — The Sill's mechanic, and ours is worth +29% to +121%.
